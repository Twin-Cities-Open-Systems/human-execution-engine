       section:  1  hee-cache-prune -- prune stale entries from the real hee-*
       disk caches (hee-filter's repo-visibility  cache,  hee-git-merge's  PR-

       unprivileged-by-design mechanism (make -f tooling/bootstrap.mk install-
       cron), not a one-off manually-copied script.

       Usage: hee-cache-prune [days, default 7]
